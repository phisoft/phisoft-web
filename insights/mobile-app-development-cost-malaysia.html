How Much Does It Cost to Build a Mobile App in Malaysia? (2026 Guide)

Whether you're a tour operator in Kundasang needing a booking app or a logistics company in Kota Kinabalu managing deliveries across Sabah, understanding mobile app costs is the first step. Here's a practical breakdown based on real projects delivered in East Malaysia.

Cost by app type (East Malaysia pricing)

App TypeFeaturesEst. Cost (MYR)Timeline

What drives the cost?

  • Platform choice: Building for both iOS and Android costs more than a single platform. Cross-platform frameworks like Flutter can reduce costs by 30-40% while maintaining quality.
  • Design complexity: Custom UI/UX design adds RM8,000–20,000 but significantly improves user adoption and satisfaction.
  • Backend infrastructure: Apps that need user accounts, real-time data sync, or payment processing require a secure backend — typically RM15,000–50,000.
  • Third-party integrations: Connecting to payment gateways (YONO, Touch 'n Go), accounting software (AutoCount, SQL), or LHDN e-invoicing adds RM5,000–15,000 per integration.

Malaysia vs. overseas developers

Hiring a Sabah or KL-based team typically costs more upfront than outsourcing to India or Vietnam, but you get better communication, local compliance knowledge, and easier long-term maintenance. For Malaysian businesses dealing with local regulations and payment systems, a local developer is almost always the better long-term investment.

Ongoing costs to budget for

  • App Store fees: Apple charges USD 99/year; Google Play charges a one-time USD 25 fee.
  • Server & hosting: RM200–2,000/month depending on user volume.
  • Maintenance & updates: Budget 15-20% of initial development cost annually for bug fixes, OS updates, and feature improvements.
