# PHISOFT 2026 WEBSITE REVAMP SPECIFICATION

**Website:** https://www.phisoft.my/  
**Company:** Phi Software Sdn Bhd  
**Primary market:** Sabah and Malaysia  
**Primary objective:** Reposition Phisoft from a general software development company into an **AI + Software Engineering Partner for AI-Powered Business Transformation**.

---

# 1. Purpose of This Document

This document is the implementation brief for revamping the current Phisoft website.

It should be used by:

- management
- marketing
- UI/UX designers
- frontend developers
- SEO specialists
- copywriters
- sales team

The website must communicate a clear evolution:

```text
OLD PERCEPTION
Software Development Company
        ↓
NEW PERCEPTION
AI + Software Engineering Partner
        ↓
LONG-TERM POSITION
AI-Powered Business Transformation Partner
```

The goal is **not** to make Phisoft look like a new AI startup.

The goal is to show that Phisoft already has the software engineering, integration, business systems and long-term support capabilities required to make AI useful inside real businesses.
